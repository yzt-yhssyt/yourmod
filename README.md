# Your Mod Enchantments

> 6 个自定义附魔：日光 / 月光修复、窃取者、突刺、腹泻、探索者。
>
> Six custom enchantments: sunlight and moonlight mending, stealer, thrust, diarrhea and explorer.

**Minecraft 1.21.1 · Fabric · 作者 yzt**

## 简介

六个各自独立的小附魔，都能在附魔台和村民交易里正常出现：有的负责修装备，有的改变战斗节奏，还有一个纯粹是恶作剧。

## 特性

- 日光修复：白天每 20 tick 修复主手或副手 1 / 2 / 4 点耐久（按等级）
- 月光修复：夜晚做同样的事，与日光修复互为昼夜
- 窃取者：攻击非玩家、非 Boss 目标时，有 5% + 3%/级 概率从它的战利品表里偷一件物品直接到手
- 突刺：按键向前突进 5 格（II 级 10 格），每格消耗 1 点耐久，冷却按距离计算
- 腹泻：附在胸甲或护腿上，被攻击时 8% / 15% 概率让攻击者腹泻 10 秒（移速 −30%，每秒掉血）
- 探索者：附在头盔或靴子上，准星 16 格内的怪物被标记并减速 10% / 15%，同时自身获得 10% / 15% 移速
- 全部附魔已加入附魔台与村民交易标签

## 安装

1. 安装 Minecraft 1.21.1 与 Fabric Loader（≥ 0.16.14）
2. 安装依赖：Fabric API（必需）
3. 把 `yourmod-1.0.0.jar` 放进 `mods` 文件夹

## English

Six small, self-contained enchantments that show up through enchanting tables and villager trades: some maintain your gear, some change how fights flow, and one is pure mischief.

- Sunlight Mending: repairs 1 / 2 / 4 durability on your main or off hand every second during the day, scaled by level
- Moonlight Mending: the same at night — the two cover a full day
- Stealer: hitting a non-player, non-boss target has a 5% + 3% per level chance to steal one item straight from its loot table
- Thrust: press the key to dash 5 blocks forward (10 at level II), costing 1 durability per block, with distance-based cooldowns
- Diarrhea: on a chestplate or leggings, 8% / 15% chance to give your attacker diarrhea for 10 s (−30% speed, damage over time)
- Explorer: on a helmet or boots, monsters within 16 blocks of your crosshair are marked and slowed by 10% / 15%, while you gain 10% / 15% movement speed
- All enchantments are wired into the enchanting table and villager trade tags

### Installation

1. Minecraft 1.21.1 with Fabric Loader (≥ 0.16.14)
2. Dependencies: Fabric API (required)
3. Drop `yourmod-1.0.0.jar` into your `mods` folder
